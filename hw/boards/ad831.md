# AD831 mixer board

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Part | AD831, doubly balanced active mixer |
| RF / LO bandwidth | to 500 MHz (RF IP3 spec'd to 400 MHz) |
| IF output | to 250 MHz differential current, or DC to over 200 MHz single-ended voltage |
| LO drive | -10 dBm |
| Performance | IP3 +24 dBm, P1dB +10 dBm, NF 10.3 dB @ 70 MHz, 14 dB @ 150 MHz |
| Supply (chip) | +/-4.5 to +/-5.5 V, or 9 to 11 V single; 100 to 125 mA |
| Common module | 33 x 33 mm, single 9 to 11 V supply, DC-isolation capacitors on RF / IF / LO, IF gain set by a resistor between OUT and VFB |

## Seen in the photo ([photos/ad831-module.jpg](../photos/ad831-module.jpg))
Silkscreen `AD831_MIX Freq:1-500MHz`. SMA `RFin` (one end), `LOin` (side), `IFout` (opposite end of RFin); two pads `GND` and `VCC` with grey leads soldered on. So it is the single-supply module (datasheet single supply: 9 to 11 V), not a bare chip. The supply voltage actually applied is not visible: measure it.
